# 7 The ThinqBridge Logo

**Where Clear Thinking Meets Forward Movement**

**A logo built to express the connection between intelligence and execution.**

The ThinqBridge logo was designed to reflect the two ideas at the heart of the business: clear thinking and forward movement. Its form combines the structure of a brain with the strength of a bridge, symbolising the connection between intelligence and execution. For me, that is what ThinqBridge is about — not just having good ideas, but helping businesses carry those ideas across complexity and turn them into something real, useful, and built to move forward.
